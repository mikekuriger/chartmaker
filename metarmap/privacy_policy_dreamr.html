Privacy Policy – Dreamr

Effective Date: April 30, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect your first name and email address during registration. This information is used solely to create and manage your Dreamr account. We do not sell, share, or transmit personally identifiable information to third parties.

2. Location Data

Dreamr does not access or collect any GPS or location-based data.

3. Network and External Communication

Dreamr connects to external servers at dreamr.zentha.me to store dreams, generate AI analyses, and retrieve associated content. All communications are encrypted using HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services

Dreamr may interact with the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI – Used to analyze submitted dreams and generate creative responses based on user input.
  • Image Generation APIs – Used optionally to render dream-related images. These requests are anonymous and do not include personally identifiable information.

Dreamr contains no ads, uses no analytics tools, and includes no third-party SDKs that track or collect user data.

5. Local Storage and Offline Use

Dream data (including AI responses and images) may be cached locally on your device to support offline access. You can delete this data anytime via app settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Data Retention

Your submitted dreams and associated data are retained on our servers until deleted by you, or upon account deletion. You may contact us at any time to request full data deletion.

7. Children’s Privacy

Dreamr is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have mistakenly collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated through the app or on the app store listing. Continued use of Dreamr after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
