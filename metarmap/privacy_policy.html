Privacy Policy – Metar Map Plus

Effective Date: April 30, 2025

1. Information Collection

Metar Map Plus does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

2. Location Data

If you allow location access, the app uses your device’s GPS to:

  • Show and/or follow your position on the map.

Your location data is used only on your device and is never shared or uploaded.

3. Network and External Data

The app accesses external sources (e.g., NOAA, FAA) to download:

  • Weather data (METARs, TAFs)
  • Temporary Flight Restrictions (TFRs)
  • Sectional charts and navigation data

These requests may be logged by the data provider’s servers (not by us).

4. Third-Party Services

Metar Map Plus may optionally interface with:

  • Stratux (a local network device for ADS-B and GPS data). No data is sent to the internet.

The app contains no ads, no analytics, and does not use third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

5. Data Storage

Downloaded data (e.g., charts, weather info) is stored locally on your device to support offline use. You may clear it anytime through your device settings or app interface.

6. Children’s Privacy

Metar Map Plus is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect data from them.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. Updates will be posted within the app or its store listing.
